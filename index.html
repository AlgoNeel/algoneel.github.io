Trading Framework

Rule-based market structure analysis prioritizing capital preservation.

A rule-based XAU/USD trader who prioritizes market structure, liquidity, and capital preservation over frequent trading.

Core Principles of My Trading Framework:

  • Higher-Timeframe Analysis: Identifying overall market bias on higher timeframes to ensure entries align with dominant trends.
  • Market Structure: Analyzing key structural points, including higher highs, lower lows, breakouts, and momentum shifts.
  • TJL (Trading Journey Levels): Using predefined TJL zones to identify high-probability continuation setups and reaction areas.
  • Quasimodo Levels (QML): Incorporating Quasimodo patterns to identify possible reversals and institutional turning points.
  • RBS & SBR Zones: Paying close attention to Resistance-Become-Support (RBS) and Support-Become-Resistance (SBR) levels.
  • Liquidity Analysis: Studying liquidity pools, stop hunts, and market imbalances to understand next price movements.
  • Supply and Demand: Locating institutional supply and demand zones representing strong buying and selling interest.
  • Fibonacci Retracements: Using Fibonacci retracement levels to refine entries and identify potential reversal zones.
  • Confirmation-Based Execution: Requiring clear confirmation from price action and structure before opening any trade.
  • Risk-to-Reward Management: Risking 30 pips to target moves of 100 pips with strict position sizing.
I believe capital preservation comes before profit. I'd rather miss an opportunity than take a trade that doesn't meet my rules.
