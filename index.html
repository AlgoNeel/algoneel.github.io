Technical Analysis & Playbook

03 Execution Setups
TJL SETUP
Trendline Joint Level
  1. Mark levels on 15M & 1H timeframes.
  2. Wait for price to reach target level.
  3. Select entry timeframe based on zone size:
    • <20–25 pips: 1M confirmation
    • 25–80 pips: 5M confirmation
    • >80 pips: 1M CHoCH rule
  4. Confirmation: Body engulfs previous candle body.
  5. Wait for candle close before entering.
Max SL: 35 pips Min TP: 100 pips

LQS SETUP
Liquidity Sweep
  1. Use 1H, 4H & 1D for bias and levels.
  2. Wait for price to sweep previous candle high/low.
  3. Direct entry on nearest 68.80% Fib or S/D candle.
  4. Or wait for fresh sweep confirmation (Buy: Bearish candle / Sell: Bullish candle engulfing).
  5. Or enter upon price tapping new S/D zone.
Max SL: 35 pips Min TP: 100 pips
